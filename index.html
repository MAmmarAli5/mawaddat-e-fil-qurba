About Mawaddat-e-Fil-Qurba

Mawaddat-e-Fil-Qurba Online Learning Platform is dedicated to spreading the teachings of the Holy Quran and Ahlul Bayt (A.S.).

Students can attend online courses, participate in examinations, view results and receive educational, Islamic and Azadari updates.
